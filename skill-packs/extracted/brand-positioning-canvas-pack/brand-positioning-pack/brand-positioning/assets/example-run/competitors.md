## Niche read

The fuller pool confirms the v1 shape and sharpens it. The niche default is a **framework breakdown delivered from a seated office/studio "yap" set, with a greenscreen of the example reel or an analytics card as the second most common backdrop** — 42% of the 118 analyzed competitor videos are breakdowns and 36% are office-yap; add the numbered list and the how-to and three families cover 70% of everything published. Hooks are overwhelmingly "never ever do X without Y" trap lines, contrarian one-liners, "[N] things that are working right now" list promises and "this got [huge number] because of one thing" reveals, and nearly every video closes with a comment-keyword lead magnet. The tactical camp (personalbrandlaunch, devinjatho, rico, bryanfahim, briarcochran, peter.visuals, sam.gaudet) has become more, not less, homogeneous with more data: they share the same levers (post-hook line, title card, trial reels, repost the winner, share rate over likes) and the same proof style (a screenshot of Insights, a 9K-vs-9M comparison). The strategy camp (orate.x, theharryneedham, house.of.ag, tomnoske, shwinnabegobrand) still talks offers, positioning and archetypes without showing execution, and iamjadenly sits between them as the one center channel building named brand frameworks.

What the deeper data corrects from v1: (1) **retention and delivery, not hooks, is the most crowded cell** — six different channels run a "retention & delivery × framework breakdown" video (Hook Bridge, Promise Stacking, timer trick, anchor method, tonality, voice as habit), while pure hook-writing videos are rarer than the captions suggested (7 of 118) and three of the hook videos are the same "rewrite a famous hook" move. (2) **Storytelling is close to empty in the competitor pool** — only 4 of 118 videos, all from orate.x and personalbrandlaunch, and none of them a worked framework; Kallaway alone has 12, which makes "storytelling & scripting × framework breakdown / how-to" his single most defensible cell rather than a lane he shares. (3) **AI-workflow content is no longer just tool demos** — iamjadenly (Claude carousel series), orate.x (four brand documents for AI), sam.gaudet (Claude Code), bryanfahim (Claude + composio research) and personalbrandlaunch (3 levels of AI) all touched it in the last two months, so the "AI × content" lane is being entered from the creator side; what none of them do is Kallaway's data loop (analyze → pattern → make → read the numbers), which stays unique in the pool. (4) The **case-study format is used by 7 channels but almost always on someone else's video** (peter.visuals' bowl clip, rico's client title card, harry's Jun Yuh purchase, orate's IKEA); only jessijean (then-vs-now voice) and bryanfahim (13M → 1M repost) use their own channel as the specimen, so first-party receipts remain open.
