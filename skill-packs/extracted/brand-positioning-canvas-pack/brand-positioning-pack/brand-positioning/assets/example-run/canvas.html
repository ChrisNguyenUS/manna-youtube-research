Headline frame · contested on words, fresh on proof

Data-driven growth that stays creative

They sayPost more, be authentic, copy proven formats.I sayUse data to decide what to make; use creativity to make it. Outliers are the only honest demand signal.
  • Outliers, not averages, tell you what to make next
  • Trial reels are guessing with extra steps — screen first
  • AI is a research engine, not a writer

Proof: 0 → ~450K YouTube in under two years; 111K IG in months — on this workflow. Six channels entered AI × content in 60 days, all tool-first; 2 of 118 competitor videos show the creator's own numbers.

Frame 2 · fresh

Application content beats education

They sayEducational content wins — list the tips, give the framework.I sayEducation split into eduslop, tactical education and application content. Showing the skill in the video is the only thing copy culture can't copy.
  • A live build outranks a listicle on trust
  • Case studies are a format, not a topic

Proof: "Application Content" reel — 195K, 7.3×, 5,374 comments. 7 channels use case studies, almost always on someone else's video; 0 of 118 are a live build on their own channel.

Frame 3 · contested

Aim smaller to grow bigger

They sayGo broad for reach — "you are the niche."I sayOne viral Ring-5 video teaches the algorithm the wrong audience. Speak to one buyer; it expands you itself.
  • Views from the wrong people are a cost
  • Attract rich viewers, not broke viewers

Proof: 367 short-form videos — narrow converted, broad got views. Adjacent: rico's "steakhouse," iamjadenly's "cult 10,000" — yours has the mechanism.

Frame 4 · fresh — sole occupant

Storytelling is a ladder, not a talent

They sayYou're a natural storyteller or you're not.I saySix levels — Reporter → Maestro — any owner can climb with a process; it's how boring topics become addictive.
  • Stakes make the viewer feel it's about them
  • Comprehension is a storytelling skill

Proof: Story Ladder — 127K, 6.5×, 1,750 comments. Deep pass: 4 storytelling videos across all 17 competitors, none a worked framework; you have 12+.

4 frames · 5 takes vaulted · 12 common beliefs on the boardLoudest belief in the niche after the deep pass: "retention is won right after the hook" (6 channels — Hook Bridge, tonality, the anchor). Your counter: retention is a storytelling problem, not a line-two problem.
