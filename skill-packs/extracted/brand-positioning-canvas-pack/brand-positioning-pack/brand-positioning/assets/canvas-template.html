PILLAR 2

Core Contrarian Beliefs

The 3–5 macro frames the brand hangs on — a new solution to an old problem, positioned against what the crowd says. Same opinions as everyone else = a smaller derivative of whoever said them first.

Headline frame

Data-driven growth that stays creative

They sayGrowth is vibes — be creative, post a lot, hope. I sayUse data to decide what to make; use creativity to make it. That's the goldilocks.
  • Outliers, not averages, tell you what to make next
  • Study your niche's top 50 before you script anything
  • A watchlist beats a mood board

Proof: 0 → ~450K YouTube subs in under two years running this exact workflow.

Frame 2

Aim smaller to grow bigger

They sayGo broad — a bigger audience means more reach. I sayBroad content confuses the algorithm. Speak to one person and it expands you outward itself.
  • One viral Ring-5 video teaches the algorithm the wrong audience
  • The bullseye, not the follower count, is the strategy

Proof: 367 short-form videos; the narrow ones converted, the broad ones just got views.

Frame 3

Views are the receipt, not the product

They sayMore followers = more money. I sayMoney per follower is the score. Efficient conversion beats mainstream fame for a business.
  • Pick your game before you pick your content
  • Proof aimed at the buyer converts; proof aimed at everyone impresses

Proof: a 30K-buyer audience outearns a 1M-scroller audience on every offer I've run.

14 takes vaultedVault rule: watch something in your space → disagree violently? → write topic · their belief · your take. Repeat forever; re-cluster every ~20 entries.
