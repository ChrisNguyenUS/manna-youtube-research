# Benchmark: @kallawaymarketing (Instagram), Sep 2026

The channel this skill was built on. Use as a comparison point only — every
channel gets its own recipe from its own data.

Two runs, 15 days apart, so you can see what moves when a channel keeps posting:

| | Sep 2 run (v1.0) | Sep 17 re-run (v1.1) |
|---|---|---|
| reels pulled / analyzable | 118 / 109 | 133 / 119 |
| credits spent | 0 (captions are the script) | 0 |
| baseline median · pooled | 11.8 · 15.0 | 11.6 · 14.2 |
| est. leads on views | ~93.6K on 6.2M | ~101.5K on 7.1M |
| no-CTA control | 0.2–0.4 per 1K | 0.4 per 1K (n=3) |
| caption-only podcast clips | 6.0 per 1K | 6.8 per 1K (n=11) |

**Recipe the data picked on Sep 17** (all four survived confound checks):

| lever | lift | median with / without | n with / without | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| keyword mentioned 2+ times | 1.84× | 20.1 / 11.0 | 21 / 98 | < 0.001 |
| asset magnet (doc, database, skill, link) vs video | 1.50× | 13.6 / 9.1 | 69 / 50 | < 0.001 |
| "…and I'll send it **through**" vs "send you / to you" | 1.46× | 15.4 / 10.5 | 47 / 72 | < 0.001 |
| AI-usable ("feed this into Claude", a skill file) | 1.24× | 14.1 / 11.4 | 17 / 102 | 0.021 |

Ladder: 0 of 4 → 7.3 · 1 → 11.4 · 2 → 15.4 · 3 → 18.7 · 4 → 18.1 (top rung is 2
videos; treat 3-of-4 as the real top). Trend p < 0.001.

**What changed between the runs**

- The imperative opener ("Comment X" vs "If you want…") was the third lever on
  Sep 2 (1.29×, p = 0.02). On Sep 17 it is 1.13×, p = 0.15 — same direction, no
  longer significant — and the picker replaced it with AI-usable. Honest read:
  AI-usable is mostly "the magnet is a Claude skill file" (that magnet group
  converts at 16.2 vs doc 12.6, video 9.1); it is the asset lever wearing a
  different hat, not a fifth idea.
- Bookend (hook + close) is 1.62× on its own but overlaps 2+ mentions ≥ 60%, so
  only one of them enters the recipe (by design).
- The 14 videos posted after Sep 2 ran at median 10.9 — below the channel — and
  the split directions on those 14 alone matched the channel for every lever
  with 3+ videos on each side (2+ mentions 1.54×, send-through 2.25×, asset
  magnet 1.76×, my/our 0.62×). Three features flipped on the new 14 only ("has
  a number", keyword ≤ 5 letters, 3+ power words) with 4–8 videos per side —
  under the floor; on the whole channel all three stay at 0.96–0.99×.

**Null results, both runs** (within ±15% of baseline, p > 0.15): CTA length,
final-line length, power-word count, "free", "exact/exactly", quantified
magnet, "with examples", "I put together" effort framing, ALL-CAPS keyword,
keyword length, totality words, "named magnet".

**Leaning negative, not yet significant**: "my/our" possessive 0.70× (p = 0.19),
up from flat on Sep 2. "The other N" remainder tease 0.84× — really the video
magnet it usually rides with. "My system / framework" is the weakest magnet
(7.2) — it reads as a pitch.

**Other observations**: authority-style hooks pull the most leads per view
(13.9); shorter scripts convert better (ρ = −0.34); views and conversion are
positively correlated (ρ = +0.31), so the per-1K metric is not fully
view-neutral on this channel.

**What surprised the creator**: adjectives didn't matter, architecture did. The
biggest single win was simply asking twice.
